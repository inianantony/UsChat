📋 How to export your chats:
  1. Open WeChat 3.9.12.15 and log in
  2. Wait for your chats to fully sync
  3. Close WeChat completely (so data is saved)
  4. Reopen WeChat and keep it running
  5. Click the decrypt button below!

💡 Why close first? WeChat saves pending messages when you close it.
